My VCV Rack plugin — [BerserkAudio](https://library.vcvrack.com/BerserkAudio) version 2.0.1 — consists of three modules: Scrambler, Xenizer, and XenScribe.

Scrambler — Sample-reordering effect. Also runs on the 4ms MetaModule.

Xenizer — Microtonal Quantizer with 8 built-in scales. The scales were generated via my [Mass Spectrum to Audio Converter](https://mass-spectrum-to-audio-converter.onrender.com/), specifically by hitting the random button on the microtonal keyboard section and exporting the .scl. The scales are named after various compounds, but the numbers aren't actually derived from spectrum data or anything, they just made cool names. Also runs on the 4ms MetaModule.
